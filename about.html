About Ceylon Drop

A story rooted in the lush hills of Sri Lanka — where every capsule carries generations of tea heritage.

Our Story

Ceylon Drop was born from a deep love for Ceylon Green Tea and a desire to make it accessible to the modern world. Our family has been cultivating tea in the highland regions of Sri Lanka for three generations.

The idea was simple: preserve the authentic taste and health benefits of our premium green tea — but make it effortless. No brewing, no tea bags, no mess. Just drop, stir, and drink.

Every capsule is made from 100% pure whole-leaf Ceylon Green Tea, carefully processed to retain its natural antioxidants, L-Theanine, and EGCG. Nothing added. Nothing taken away.
