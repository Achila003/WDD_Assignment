Why Green Tea?

Ceylon Green Tea has been celebrated for centuries for its remarkable health properties.

Rich in Antioxidants

Ceylon Green Tea is packed with powerful antioxidants that help protect your cells from damage caused by free radicals.

L-Theanine Support

L-Theanine promotes calm focus and mental clarity without the jittery effects of coffee — perfect for productive days.

Natural Energy Boost

A gentle, sustained energy boost from natural caffeine — no crash, no anxiety. Just clean, focused energy.

Heart Health

Regular consumption of green tea is associated with improved cardiovascular health and lower bad cholesterol levels.

Natural EGCG

EGCG (Epigallocatechin gallate) is the most potent catechin in green tea, known for its anti-inflammatory properties.

Metabolism Support

Green tea has been shown to support healthy metabolism and assist with weight management when combined with an active lifestyle.
